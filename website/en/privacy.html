Privacy policy

BigPad processes files exclusively on your device and does not collect personal data.

1. Overview

BigPad is a native, open-source Windows application. It requires neither a user account nor an internet connection and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

2. File processing

Files opened or edited with BigPad remain on the user's computer. BigPad does not upload file contents to external servers or transmit them to the developer or third parties.

3. Local settings

BigPad may store preferences such as language, theme, font family, and font size locally in the user's profile. These settings are not transmitted and can be deleted by removing the application's settings.

4. Telemetry and external services

BigPad uses no telemetry, analytics services, advertising networks, cookies, or cloud synchronization. The application does not independently connect to external services.

5. Website and downloads

This static website sets no cookies and includes no analytics or tracking services. When visiting the website or downloading through GitHub, the respective hosting provider and GitHub may process technical access data under their own privacy policies.

6. Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be submitted through the public BigPad project on GitHub. This policy may be updated if the application or its data processing changes.
